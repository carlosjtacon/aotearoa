Aotearoa New Zealand

We are Carlos and Alix and here they are our travelling adventures in New Zealand. We share each stage of our trip in our Logbook with the places we've visited, the Photos we've taken, the Tracks we've walked.

We also try to share our Knowledge about New Zealand - like food we harvested or radio stations we found on the way. Alix travels with her Sketchbook where she draws the landscapes, people and things we find on the way. And we also have here our Travel Tools in case they might be handy for someone else.
