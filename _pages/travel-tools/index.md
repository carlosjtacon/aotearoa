# Travel Tools and Websites

## Cool tools and websites to use while traveling.

### [Rankers](https://www.rankers.co.nz)
Best place to check everything travel related. They also have a super cool printed map.

### [Swimming Pools Map](https://arcg.is/0O5Xfe)
To take a shower on the road.

### [Lord of the Rings Filming Locations](https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-go/lord-of-the-rings-locations/) [and Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/viewer?mid=1L6-fZ_OAS3VhY41tsZlkKATFlsI&ll=-40.73731962237572%2C174.91310301619217&z=7)
We will try to see some of this ones as Lord of the Rings fans :D

### [DOC Showers Map](https://www.carlosjtacon.com/aitools/doc_showers/)
Custom tool to check DOC campsites showers. It's only 10 hot showers in the country :(

### [Budget Planner](https://www.carlosjtacon.com/aitools/budget_planner/)
Check our monies.

## There is not one map app to rule them all.

### [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps)
Sadly there is no competition to check services and ratings.

### [OsmAnd Maps](https://osmand.net)
Drive offline and plan the route ahead. All the roadtrip tracks on the website come from here.

### [Mapout](https://mapout.app)
To plan tramping, cycling and nature related tracks.
