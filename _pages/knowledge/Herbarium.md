We are learning about native plants and trees in New Zealand, many of them have traditional uses that we have discovered. Here are some of the ones we've used or want to use.

## Harakeke

As known as New Zealand Flax it's the plant Māori used to weave. They used to make rope and thread separating the fibers from the green with a mussel shell. The leaves can also be used to do braids or other shapes.

## Kawakawa

Kawakawa is a plant from the same family as pepper, it's quite aromatic and has been traditionally used in māori kai. We can find kawakawa quite often in native forest, as small bush or a couple of meters high. The leaves can be used to make tea and the flowers as a replacement for black pepper. We found some artisan kawakawa chocolate that was also quite good!
