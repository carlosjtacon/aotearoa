We spent the first few weeks in Auckland looking for our van. We saw a few before, but when we found the grey Toyota Hiace we now have we both loved it. After a few more days we finally got it and started travelling with it - but there was one more thing to decide, we should give it a name!

Kōruru

  • 1. (verb) to be overcast, cloudy.
  • 2. (verb) to be wrinkled, puckered.
  • 3. (noun) shadow.

We thought about some other names before, but we were reading the māori dictionary and found this one. We liked it because it felt quite representative of our van and our trip.

It's old and grey like a wrinkled shadow, and New Zealand is known as a cloudy country and called Aotearoa: the long white cloud.

We found out later that a koruru is the carving that is usually at the top of the marae meeting house, as a representation of the tribe's ancestor, when this carving has round eyes. Koru is a spiral shape inspired by the unfurl of the silver fern fronds, symbolising new beginnings, growth, strength, and peace. Ruru is the New Zealand small owl. Many different symbols.

After travelling a bit with it in Northlands we decided to stop back in Auckland to do some upgrades. We bought the van with everything installed, but we wanted a nicer battery, a stationary solar panel and an awning. Over time we also installed a new shelf, kauri locks for the cabinets and a new tray for the front middle seat, doing some changes and making it our home. The Toyota logo is also back! We found it in a car yard.
