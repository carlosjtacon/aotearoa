Some local FM radio stations that we've encountered in a way or another.

### 88.2 Whananaki FM
Run from the local shop in Whananaki in Northlands, as known as the best general store in the universe.
The owner was the happiest person, really nice guy.

### 96.9 Tumeke FM
We saw a billboard on the road and looked for the station with the van radio. We listened while in Whakatāne and found really cool Maori songs:
- Raumati - Corella
- Mō Āke Tonu - Stan Walker
- ... 🎶
