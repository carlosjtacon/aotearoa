## aotearoa.carlosjtacon.com

Logbook, knowledge and utilities from traveling New Zealand.

- Logbook posts will have an entry per stage of the trip, roadtrip days or stays in different places. It will have attached the gpx of the stage from osmand maps, with  the road and waypoints. It will also have linked walks and tramps done in that stage from mapout, and list of photos of the ones taken with the !boring camera app.

- Knowledge pages should be things we learn about New Zealand from any topic: gather our own food on the sea, types of birds we find... 

- Utilities pages are tools and things we can use in the trip to make life easier: DOC showers, Van battery life calculator...
