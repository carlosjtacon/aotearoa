Let's go to the very top of New Zealand!

We left Maitai in the morning and drove all the way up, walked around Cape Reinga. It was really feeling like the end of the world and cool to see where the oceans meet. We walked down the endless beach and we were alone, just us and tuatuas. Really cool to see the micro environments in the sand, looking like miniature worlds.

After we drove down to the giant sand dunes that were really impressive, we could see the sand transforming into rocks.

We ended the day in a campsite in 90 miles beach, hosted by a cool german lady.
