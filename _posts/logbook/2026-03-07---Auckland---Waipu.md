Our first trip in New Zealand. We started in Waipu and stayed a few days in Uretiti beach campsite (with hot water showers! we will discover after how exclusive this is). We had sun and good weather, so we went to explore around :)

Dramatic skies, endless beaches, pancake rocks and nice forest areas.

{% include film.html name="202607_CO_NZ01_Auckland_Whanganui_013-5" %}

Waipu is a small town with scottish heritage - we even had a Caledonian Park as we used to in London! - and everyone is really nice there.

We met Todd in the garage and he recommended so many walks around and also told us how to [cook the shells we could find in the beach](/knowledge/Cooking%20Tuatua%20and%20Pipis). McLeods brewery had really good beers and pizzas.
