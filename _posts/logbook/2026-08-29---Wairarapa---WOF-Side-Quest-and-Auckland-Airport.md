The first thing we need to do before starting our trip with Alix parents it to get the new warrant of fitness for Kōruru. We went back to Douglas in Rotorua, because we were really happy they were the only ones that fixed the ugly noise we had in the van, and we're going north anyways! Sanctuary Farm was on the way, so we also passed by and said hi, with all the new born lambs and baby goat salt and pepper.

We spent the day in Rotorua and at the end of the day we had the new WOF! Easy. Before leaving the area we spent a couple of days in Whirinaki Forest, which is a really cool jurassic looking forest, with waterfalls, rivers, magnificent trees and cool moss.

After that we slowly climbed our way up to Auckland, spending some nights on the way. We found a very cool free camp where we could play disc-golf with the frisbees we already had on the van - we finally discovered what were they for! We walked a bit the rainy Maungatautari, and spent the last day in Raglan watching and taking pictures of the pro surfers in the beach, it's a cool small town.

We picked up Alix parents in the airport, it's really nice they came to visit - we had some hours to visit a very small amount of Auckland and tomorrow we will start the roadtrip together - it's gonna be cool! 😄
