After Alix parents left we ended up staying around Christchurch for a few days, recharging batteries at the library and meeting for dinner with Quentin and Léa - yes, we found them again!

We wanted to go visit Sophie and Igor to Ōamaru - we've been chatting since we landed and now we are close by. Sophie is the daughter from Alix grandparent's friends, and she's been explaining us many things about NZ, so it was really nice to meet them and spend a couple of days together. Thanks for hosting us, teaching us about birds and bringing us to see penguins!

But first we decided to go to Mt Sunday: which is Edoras and Rohan in The Lord of the Rings, it was a really cool landscape, a small mountain surrounded by big peaks full of snow. We camped there, and spent a morning painting. I'm happy to know I'm not painting worse than when I was 10 years old, but Alix made a beautiful painting of the area, close to Lake Emma.

We went through Geraldine and Timaru, then arrived to Ōamaru. It's the steampunk town, everything spins around the steampunk style it's cool and interesting to see, including the HQ with Mad Max style props.
