We planned to go back towards Kaueranga river on the other side, to walk the tunnels at Broken Hills. But first we wanted to go to the Cathedral Cove beach and Hot Water beach. When we arrived to the Cathedral Cove village it was raining like crazy so we skipped it.

We met a few Spanish friends in a cafe and decided to drive together to Hot Water beach - even if it's rainy we can warm up. The water was literally boiling and you can see the bubbles - the way to regulate the temperature was mixing the hot spring with the sea water - advanced science.

We drove down and passed by Mount Paku for lunch, to finish at Broken Hills.
