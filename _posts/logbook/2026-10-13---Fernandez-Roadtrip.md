My family came to visit and we spent almost 3 weeks travelling together - we did the full round trip to the south island! It was fun, we saw tons of things and finished really tired but happy to see them and spend some time together.

### Day 01 - Pick up in Christchurch
The first day we picked them up in the airport and went together to pick up their van, after that groceries and spent the night at the campsite in Christchurch, we didn't have time to visit anything but we cooked a nice BBQ dinner together trying New Zealand finest meat.

### Day 02 - Christchurch - Mt Sunday
The main door of the van was stuck - lucky we didn't start travelling! It took a while but we could get it fixed before we started rolling. We drove to Mt Sunday with bad weather so we didn't stop on the way.

### Day 03 - Mt Sunday - Ōamaru
The bad weather abandoned us and gave us a really nice morning to walk Mt Sunday - surrounded by cows on the way, it was sunny and the views were spectacular. Everyone managed to reach the top and even do some yoga at the tip. As we started to travel again we saw the rain approaching, we were lucky!

We stopped in Geraldine to buy some cheese and provisions, and finished the day in Ōamaru's seaside campsite. We could walk to the penguins area at sunset, we saw some funky seals but we couldn't see any penguins... until we were going back and all the penguins were lining up to cross the road back to their burrows, they are extremely cute.

### Day 04 - Ōamaru - Dunedin
In the morning we visited a bit of Ōamaru's old city centre and the botanic gardens. The gardens were beautiful with a lot of blooming flowers, so we spent some time taking some cool pictures. We finished the day in Dunedin, doing a small walking uphill/downhill tour, and taking some beer and dinner out in the traditional breweries.


### Day 05 - Dunedin - Milford
We had to keep moving since we have the Milford Sound cruise booked for the next day. We had a long drive but we stopped in Katiki Point to see some more seals - we were looking for the yellow eyed penguins but we couldn't find them. We also stopped in Te Anau on the way, we ate some pies and walked to the aviary where we saw big Kakariki. We stopped in a campsite on the way to Milford, roasted some marshmallows and got eaten by sandflies.

### Day 06 - Milford - Te Anau
Waking up early is hard but it's not too bad because we are going to see Milford Sound. We drove early to get to the first cruise. The road is very cool, with lots of nice views. The cruise is really nice too, we saw it cloudy one way and sunny when we returned - big waterfalls that go straight to the sea, fiordlands penguins and dramatic landscapes.

We climbed up to the viewpoint and after drove back to Te Anau, stopping in other nice lakes and viewpoints on the way back, we even saw a little shag nesting, and saw south island robin for the first time. In Te Anau we had a campsite with hot pools, we did the laundry and also tried to go out for dinner... but it was too many things so the only thing we found open at the time was a chinese takeaway.

### Day 07 - Te Anau - Arrowtown
Another driving day, we stopped for lunch in Kingston, with really nice views of the lake and the mountains. After that we continued driving, skipping Queenstown and ending the day in Arrowtown.  We saw the Chinese miners settlement there, we walked the picturesque streets and had some local beer and dinner in the pub.

### Day 08 - Arrowtown - Haast
To the west coast we sail - but first we stop in Wanaka to see the lake tree, not sure about its story but after a picture with the tree we go to Haast pass. We stopped on the way to see the typical viewpoints - the small walk to blue pools was quite cool, and a couple of waterfalls after we arrived in the West Coast.

We passed Haast and stopped in Ship Creek beach, where we were lucky to see the Hector dolphins having fun and swimming the waves. We slept in the possum alley at night.

### Day 09 - Haast - Fox Glacier
We will spend a few days in the West Coast so we don't really have to drive too much. We walked the Fox Glacier walk, a couple of hours passing through a really magical forest, to finish in the glacier river with views to it.

In the evening we had dinner in the Saloon bar, and after walked the glow worms walk at night, where we saw a lot of them and a wild possum.

### Day 10 - Fox Glacier - Ōkārito
It's kiwi day - there are some in Ōkārito but we probably won't see, so we stopped in the kiwi centre at Franz Joseph, where we saw the kiwi they had - and we have never seen any so close up, without a barrier, and he even called once it was really cool to see and hear close up. We also saw penguins and tuatara.

Ōkārito is really calm and nice place to visit and it has a lot of birdlife. We walked the beach and saw some local people fishing for whitebait, some black billed gulls and a white heron. We also walked the wetlands and saw some more giant jurassic ferns and trees. For dinner we tried some clams that Alix took from the beach, they were so good!

Alix and me tried to go see wild kiwis at night, but we couldn't even hear them :(

### Day 11 - Ōkārito - Hokitika
Only a week left to finish the trip! After walking a bit more in Ōkārito we drove. Our first stop was Ross where we did a short gold mining walk and saw some heritage houses. We arrived to Hokitika and spent some time in the campsite, some of us finally saw the west coast beautiful sunset.

### Day 12 - Hokitika - Fox River
We saw the town in the morning, with the idea of seeing the museum when it opened, but the museum never opened. We saw some pounamu carving shops instead, learned all the different kinds and shapes of pounamu, so many different ones. After that we also saw the museum in Punakaiki where we could learn a bit more about the pounamu, geology and local traditions in the west coast.

We saw pancake rocks again and slept in Fox River.

### Day 13 - Fox River - Tākaka
It's Sunday in Fox River and that means Sunday Market. We had coffee and breakfast there, there was live music, more pounamu carvings, artists, artisans and cake.

After that we drove all the way up to Tākaka, parked in town and had a beer in the Roots bar - which is funny because it is where I am writing this lines a few weeks after!

### Day 14 - Still around Tākaka
Our day in Golden Bay we went to the clear water Pūpū stream, and then all the way up to Wharariki beach, where we saw some sheep, camouflage crabs and took some mussels to try before dinner (best mussels I ever had). We ended the day singing along in the Mussel Inn and sleeping with the horses again in Hack and Stay.

### Day 15 - Tākaka - Wai-iti Domain
Going back towards Nelson, we drove and stopped in the different viewpoints: Kaiteriteri beach with the golden sand and turquoise water, Riwaka resurgence clear spring and fairy forest, and finishing in the Wai-iti domain where we could see the stars and learn about the southern sky constellations.

### Day 16 - Wai-iti Domain - Kaikōura
From Nelson to Kaikoura there is quite a bit of road so we stopped on the way to try real fruit ice cream in Blenheim. Pretty good in strawberry season! Once in Kaikoura we had a nice dinner in the Strawberry tree, where we tried the famous and expensive crayfish, paua and local fish.

### Day 17 - Kaikōura - Christchurch
We woke up early again for the only other booked activity - the whale watching cruise. The boat ride was great, we saw the humpback whale pretty good and really close up, they were also quite calm so we had the time to see them swimming. We also saw albatross and heaps of dolphins racing with the boat.

We saw a bit of Kaikōura peninsula before driving back to Christchurch.

### Day 18 - Lat day in Christchurch
Last day already! Time passes fast. We gave back the van in the morning, put the backpacks in the hostel and go visit Christchurch. We had really bad weather so we couldn't see much - we had lunch in the riverside and then visited some of the old college buildings, galleries and museums.

Time to say goodbye - we were really tired after this trip, but it was really really cool to see everyone and to see the whole South Island. Thanks for coming to visit us and we will see you again in the other side of the world ❤️🌍
