On the way to Mordor and the Mount Doom - in real life Tongariro and Mount Ngāruhoe. It was a real life Lord of the Rings experience. We passed by Taupō but we didn't really stopped much - we wanted to arrive in Tongariro with good weather and it was worth it!

The Tongariro crossing is a really beautiful hike, surrounded by volcanic rocks it looks like a martian landscape. We had a really clear day so we could see everything - even Taranaki in the distance.
