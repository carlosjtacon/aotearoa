We were not too far from Aoraki / Mt Cook, and it was really nice weather, so we decided to visit. From Ōamaru we went through the vanished world area, where we could see some funky rocks and whale bones. And sheep, lots of sheep.

Before going up Aoraki we stayed for a night in the Lake Pukaki, where we had a small bath in glaciar waters, it was quite refreshing. Mount Cook is beautiful, we walked a few tracks with really nice views - also with a lot of steps to go up! We couldn't reach the hut because it was snowed and we didn't have good gear. In any case we could see the mountains, the lakes and the icebergs... scenic.

On the way back we stopped in Lake Tekapo and Burkes Pass - American vibes.
