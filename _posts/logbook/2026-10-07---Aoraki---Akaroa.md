We tried some tasty jam and chutney in Geraldine's local shop and then came back to Christchurch area. My family will arrive for our roadtrip next week, so we will be waiting for them around until then... except for the weekend - we have a french festival in Akaroa! 🇫🇷 🥐 🥖

After visiting Lyttelton's old bookshop and cafés we drove to Akaroa, we took the tourist drive and drove between clouds, missing the views but not the fun. The festival was nice: we spent time with Léa and Quentin at the market, playing pétanque, watching Alix life drawing skills, having croissant for breakfast and baguette jambon beurre for lunch, trying New Zealand Raclette and finished the weekend watching a circus play - couldn't be french-er.

On our way back through the mountains we had a small breakdown, our breaks in the van got too hot and smokey so we had to replace them, getting ready for the big roadtrip!
