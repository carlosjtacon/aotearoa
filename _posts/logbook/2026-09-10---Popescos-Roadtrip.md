We picked up Alix parents in the airport and slept there, so first thing in the morning we picked up the van to start the roadtrip!

### Day 01 - Auckland to Waitomo Caves

First stop is Waitomo caves, we visited the main cave - it was really cool and impressive, including a couple of songs in the cathedral and a boat ride. After that we drove up to the natural bridge and we had the chance to walk it by night - seeing a lot more glowworms on their natural habitat and for free, so we could stop and see them close by - it was a really magical night walk. And there was a lamb running inside the hostel wearing nappies.

### Day 02 - Waitomo Caves to Rotorua

We passed by Hobbiton again, so Alix parents could visit, and they loved it! After that we walked the Blue Springs and saw the clearest water we have ever seen - this was the first recommendation we got back in Auckland, and it was really worth seeing. We finished the day closer to Rotorua, in the nice free camp with disc golf.

### Day 03 - Te Puia

We planned to spend the day in Te Puia, there is plenty to do there:
1. We walked the geothermal park, the geysers and mudpools.
2. Had hangi for lunch, some food cooked in the māori traditional way.
3. Saw kiwis walking around in their night enclosure.
4. Got a glimpse of māori traditions with the cultural experience.
5. Checked out the school where they teach carving and weaving.

It was a long māori day, and also we played disc-golf in the morning!

We planned to stay in the hot pools for the night, but it was packed and we didn't have space, so we had to improvise a new place to sleep - but that's the adventure.

### Day 04 - Rotorua to Taupō

We didn't stop too much in Taupō before so ir was a nice opportunity for us to check out new things too. We stopped in Huka falls and then took the boat to see the carving in the lake. The famous carving is not too old, but it's still impressive, one needs a lot of skill to craft it.

It was also fun to see the world greatest McDoland's with its own aircraft parking.

### Day 05 - Taupō to Tongariro and Whanganui

Tongariro is always one of the best landscapes to see in the North Island - the late winter colours were beautiful, combined with the snow at the summits. We walked to soda streams and back for lunch - we tried pies for the first time, success!

We also walked the oddly named Taranaki Falls, it was our first time too and they were really cool, passing through forest and desert, always with the good views of Ruapehu.

We drove to Whanganui for the night, to get closer to Wellington.


### Day 06 - Whanganui to Wellington

Driving morning to Wellington, stopping for chores in Waikanae. Once in Wellington we had some time to walk the city centre, the waterfront and show one of the best pubs - the LBQ.

### Day 07 - Red Rocks, Wellington CBD and Te Papa

We spent the day in Wellington, first we walked Red Rocks through a sandstorm - good we saw some New Zealand Fur Seals! After that we went to CBD and mostly spent the day visiting Te Papa and walking the city, before going to sleep early because we have the ferry really early the next day.

### Day 08 - Wellington to Nelson (Finally crossing to the South Island!)

We woke up early and took the ferry to the South Island. The crossing is not too long, but we probably spent half a day until we were finally in the South Island.

We took the scenic route to Nelson just to see all the clouds, fog and rain we had on the way. The first and probably only day with bad weather. We had mussels and fish and chips.

### Day 09 - Nelson to Golden Bay

On our way to Golden Bay we stopped in a bunch of impressive places and sands of different colours.

Starting with Kaiteriteri beach, orange sand and turquoise waters, next to it we saw the split apple rock, a perfect split sphere as a mirror to another dimension.

For lunch we stopped in the Riwaka resurgence - a place related to birth and sacred to Māori. There was a small swimming area which was the best water I could have ever dived in, so we had to take a cold plunge. Feeling better we walked up to where the river is born, a small walk with big views.

After crossing the ranges we were in Golden Bay, we were on the way to out campsite in the wild west but before we stopped in The Grove - a 20 minutes walk surrounded of skull looking rocks and giant palm trees, with a uninhabited island feeling to it.

### Day 10 - Around Golden Bay

We spent a couple of nights in Hack and Stay, Alix and Sandrine spent their morning beach horse-riding  while David and me walked the Hydro walkway - as known as the best walk in the world. Not sure it was the best but it was a nice one, next to the restored water flow to feed the power station.

We had lunch in town and spent the afternoon walking Wharariki beach, between lamb fields and dunes. It was a beautiful place, a real treasure. We also saw a wild peacock.

Before going back to the campsite we stopped for a beer in the mussel inn - really fun hippy vibes!

### Day 11 - Golden Bay to West Coast, and the Big Wave

After driving for 5 hours in the longest road without any village or house we arrived in the west coast. The views were amazing, the sea was brave with waves a couple of meters high, and the forest was dense, wild and endless.

The pancake rocks and blowholes were really cool to see, a fantasy landscape to explore.

At night we wanted to do a campfire, do some sandwiches and roast some marshmallows. Everything was great and going as planned, even some people joined us around the fire... that was before the Big Wave. No one expected it and yet she arrived fast and strong, even if the tide was lowering she reached the end of the beach, bringing foam, salt and water to our sandwiches, and killing our campfire in one strike. It was fun anyways, we'll keep the marshmallows for the next time 😄

### Day 12 - West Coast to Springfield through Arthur Pass

Another day another landscape, totally different but as beautiful as the other ones. Arthur pass is really cool, surrounded by mountains with snowed summits, but also desert planes and organic rocks.

We drove through and walked around, enjoying the views and the company.

### Day 13 - Springfield to Kaikōura

Kaikōura is a bit of a detour but we wanted to do whale watching with Alix parents, so we drove there and spent the afternoon walking the Kaikōura peninsula. We walked on the beach surrounded by seals and walked on the grass surrounded by cows. We finished the day in the Strawberry Tree, one of the nicest pubs we've been in New Zealand - we miss UK pubs!

### Day 14 - Whale Watching in Kaikōura

It's really easy to find whales and many other sea animals in Kaikōura - an old whaler town now converted to tourism. We were really lucky to see so many creatures: albatross, fur seals jumping around, dolphins and 3 humpback whales. It's a magical feeling to see this animals so close to us, it was pretty cool. And we had a free rollercoaster ride.

### Day 15 - Kaikōura to Christchurch

We spent the last day walking Christchurch, a nice city centre to walk around, with cool places to eat and drink. The botanical garden is also a nice walk, with beautiful trees and flowers, spring is coming! We had a drink in New Regent Street and dinner in a church that now is a pub, pretty cool!

-------------------------------

It was a really nice trip, we saw all the different landscapes, birds, traditions and culture of New Zealand. We had good times and laughs eating lettuce in the van, having everything for breakfast and driving together through New Zealand. Thanks for crossing the world to visit us 😊
