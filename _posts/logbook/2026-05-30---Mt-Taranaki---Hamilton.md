We took the "Forgotten World Highway" north, going towards Hamilton. Both highways going north were supposed to be very beautiful roads, so we decided to do both. There was nothing really in the way, we did a few viewpoints and old tunnels. After almost finishing the forgotten one we joined the coast through a secondary road that was even cooler, very much in the back country and we were on our own.

We arrived to the elephant rock and three sisters with low tide and a beautiful sunset, we walked through the mud to see the rocks and then back to the van to see the sunset, pretty good end of the day.

The next stop before Hamilton was Waitomo Caves, we thought it was only gonna be the main cave attraction but we found so much more - it's a beautiful forest area where there are so many hidden caves. We did a couple of walks and the highlights were the natural bridge (looks like it's going to collapse any time) and the night walk where we saw so many glow worms in the cave and the forest, felt like a magical walk.

We arrived in Hamilton and met Inés - Mario's friend who is also doing the working holiday visa! We went for lunch and ice cream, and then walked around the river, it was nice to meet her maybe we cross paths again. Hamilton gardens are the main attraction and they are really cool concept - every garden is themed so you can see a lot of different gardens through time and space.

Before the Hobbiton day we spent a couple of rainy days and nights in the area, went to another cheese factory (the best one until now! over the moon) and saw the timber museum in Putāruru.
